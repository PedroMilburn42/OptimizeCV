## STAR Format

Every behavioral answer: **Situation** (context, 1-2 sentences) →
**Task** (what needed to happen) → **Action** (what the candidate
specifically did — "I," not "we") → **Result** (measurable outcome
where possible). Grounded strictly in `data/profile/candidate-profile.md` — never
embellished, never invented.

## Tough Questions

Generic categories to prepare for; `rolesherpa-interview/SKILL.md` customizes each
with the posting's language and the candidate's actual gaps:

- **Gap questions** — "You don't have [X]." Answer pattern: acknowledge honestly, connect the closest adjacent experience, show the concrete learning path. Never claim experience that isn't there.
- **Why this company** — must reference a verified, specific fact about the company, never a generic line about "innovation" or "great culture."
- **Why leaving / gap in employment** — factual, brief, forward-looking.
- **Salary expectations** — anchor to the range in `04-job-evaluation.md`'s life-situation section, stated as a range, not a single number, unless the process requires otherwise.
- **Weakness / failure question** — a real instance with a genuine lesson, not a disguised strength.

## Questions to Ask the Interviewer

By stage:
- **Phone screen:** role scope, team structure, what a successful first 90 days looks like.
- **Technical round:** the actual tech stack in use day-to-day, how technical decisions get made, current pain points.
- **Final round:** culture, leadership style, what's changed in the last year — the last real chance to surface a deal-breaker.

Never ask something the public research already answered — it signals the candidate didn't look.

## Phone / Video Logistics

- Confirm timezone and platform in advance.
- Test camera/mic 10 minutes early for video.
- Have the submitted CV and cover letter open — the interviewer read them, every claim in the room must match what's on paper.

## Roleplay Guidelines

When the candidate wants to practice: warm-up question first, then
role-specific technical questions, then one or two behavioral questions
tied to the posting's competencies, then one tough question or
curveball. After each answer: brief feedback — what worked, what to
sharpen, which STAR example (if any) would have served better.
Calibrate feedback tone against `data/profile/behavioral-profile.md` — coach
toward the candidate's natural register, not a generic ideal.
